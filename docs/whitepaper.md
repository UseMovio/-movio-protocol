# Movio Whitepaper

## Vision

A place where characters feel alive and visitors decide the tempo. Movio imagines playful worlds that react to touch, remember small habits and stay open to anyone who wants to wander in.

## Mission

Make movement the heart of play on Solana: easy to start, rewarding to explore and honest about what is confirmed and what is still pending.
